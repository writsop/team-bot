Shan Team Bot

An internal assistant for a small team. It is not offered to the public.

What it does

Team members ask the assistant, which is powered by AI, in their team chat for training and reference materials, such as lecture slides, presentation templates, recordings and guides. The assistant finds the item in material that the larger organization the team belongs to shares with its members: a shared drive of past trainings, a presentation-template folder, and guides and documents linked from that organization's resource website.

It answers from the item's content and gives its title and link. When a team member asks, it posts a copy of a slide deck or document, or a new file built from the material, in the chat. Recordings are shared as links only.

Google account access

For this, the app uses one Google permission: read-only access to Google Drive. Google's consent screen words this as access to all of the account's Drive files, but the app is built to open and download only files inside the shared material described above. For any other item, it only checks where the item is and then refuses it.

The read-only permission does not let the app edit, delete or move anything in Google Drive, or change who a file is shared with.

Details, including the outside services that help run the assistant, are in the privacy policy.
