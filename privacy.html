Privacy policy

Shan Team Bot. Last updated October 10, 2026.

Who uses the app

Shan Team Bot is an internal assistant for one small team. It is connected to one Google account, which belongs to a member of the team. Other team members use the assistant through their team chat and never sign in to it with Google. It is not offered to the public.

What Google data the app accesses

With the account holder's consent, the app uses one Google permission for this: read-only access to Google Drive (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly). Google's consent screen describes it as "See and download all your Google Drive files". The app is built to use it only for material that the larger organization the team belongs to shares with its members: a shared drive of past trainings and its catalog lists, a presentation-template folder, and guides and documents linked from that organization's resource website, including files inside folders linked there.

It reads the names, folders and contents of files in that material only when a team member's request in the team chat needs it, such as a question about a past training or a guide, or a file they ask the assistant to make, or when the connection is set up or checked.

When a team member points the assistant to a specific Drive item, the app first reads that item's basic details (its name, type and parent folders) to confirm it is inside that material. Anything outside is refused, and its contents are never downloaded or stored.

Apart from checking the account's email address, to confirm the right account was connected, the app does not read the account's email, contacts, calendar or any other Google data.

How the data is used

  • A requested file is downloaded to the team's own computer, so the assistant can read it to answer, or use it as the base for a file a team member asks for. The copy is kept so it does not have to be downloaded again. Recordings are never downloaded; only their title, date, link and length are used.
  • The contents of a requested file, mostly the text taken from it, may be processed by the AI services that run the assistant: Anthropic's Claude, and OpenAI's Codex as a backup when Claude is unavailable. This happens only to answer team members' requests in the team chat and to make the files they ask for. The text is kept with the downloaded copy and may be used again for later requests from the team.
  • Original files are not posted in the chat. Answers, and any file a team member asks for (a newly made copy of a slide deck or document, or a file built from the material), are posted only to the team chat where they were asked for, through the team's chat app (Telegram).
  • If a team member asks by voice or asks for a spoken reply, a short spoken version of the answer, which can summarize a requested file, is turned into audio by Microsoft's online text-to-speech service.
  • The read-only permission does not let the app edit, delete or move anything in Google Drive, or change who a file is shared with.
  • Google data is not sold and not used for advertising. Besides the team's members and the people who run the assistant, it goes only to the service providers named on this page, and only so that they can run the assistant.

Storage and security

The access token is stored only on the team's own computer and is not given to anyone else. Downloaded copies, the text taken from them, and a list of the file names and links the app has seen are kept on that computer until the team removes them. The people who run the assistant may look at these, and at the assistant's logs, to fix problems or keep it secure.

Removing access and deleting copies

The account holder can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To have the stored copies deleted, email writsop@gmail.com. The team then deletes the downloaded copies, the text taken from them and the stored list of file names and links. Files already posted in the team chat stay there until they are deleted in the chat.
